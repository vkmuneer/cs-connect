How this register works

  • Every year from {{ first_year }} to {{ last_year }} has a row, whether or not it has members yet.
  • A batch coordinator is usually an alumnus of that batch who acts as its point of contact.
  • The year range comes from Departments → Batch years.
  • Coordinators for the department as a whole live under Coordinators instead.

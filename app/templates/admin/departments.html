What activating a department does

  • Its alumni can pick it when registering, and its programmes become their programme choices.
  • The directory, batch browser and coordinator pages gain a department filter.
  • Jobs, messages and placements stay college-wide — a posting from any department reaches everyone.
  • Deactivating it hides the department from new registrations; existing members keep their records.
